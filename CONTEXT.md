# Polish Club Notes

Partnership bidding notes for a Polish Club system. Opening guidelines for borderline hands are keyed to simulation-tested shape families, not to remainder patterns within those families.

## Language

**5-4 two-suiter**:
A hand with one 5-card suit and one 4-card suit. Opening guidelines are keyed to whether those two suits are majors or minors.
_Avoid_: 5431 hand (as a name for this whole family)

**5M–4M**:
A 5-4 two-suiter with a 5-card major and a 4-card major.
_Avoid_: both majors (when 5–5 is meant)

**5M–4m**:
A 5-4 two-suiter with a 5-card major and a 4-card minor.

**5m–4M**:
A 5-4 two-suiter with a 5-card minor and a 4-card major.

**5m–4m**:
A 5-4 two-suiter with a 5-card minor and a 4-card minor.
_Avoid_: both minors (when 5–5 is meant)

**5332 with major**:
A balanced-ish hand with exactly one 5-card major and 3-3-2 in the other suits. Simulation label: 5M-332.
_Avoid_: 5M-332 (in published notes)

**Remainder shape**:
How the other four cards split in a 5-4 two-suiter: 5431, 5422, or 5440. Not a separate opening-rule category; simulations found no significant open/pass difference between 5431 and 5422.
_Avoid_: 5431, 5422 (as opening-rule names)

**Proven**:
A simulation result whose 95% confidence interval excludes zero. Only a proven cell gets a hard Open in the published guidelines.

**Unproven**:
A cell whose confidence interval includes zero, or whose dataset is retracted or missing. The published decision is Pass.
_Avoid_: lean open (as a published decision)

**Position 1**:
Dealer; first to speak.

**Position 2**:
Second to speak after dealer passed. Published opening rules cover position 1 and 2 together unless the evidence bar forces a split.

**Position 3**:
Third to speak after two passes. Opening thresholds are the position 1–2 rules with every HCP requirement reduced by 1.

**QT gate**:
A Quick Trick floor used as an opening restriction. Not a default quality filter; it stays in the guidelines only where a paired test (or a proven HCP slice) supports it.
_Avoid_: QT (as a general reason an HCP floor “works”)
